The first version I'm sharing. Download **Finance Tracker Setup 1.0.0.exe** below and run it.

Windows will probably say "Windows protected your PC" because the app isn't code-signed. Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

**What's in it**

- Net worth across all your accounts and debts, in USD, GBP or EUR
- 401(k), 403(b), 457(b), IRAs, HSA, 529, brokerage, savings, checking, UK pensions and ISAs
- Employer match, IRS contribution limit warnings, and a note on how each account is taxed
- A forecast of every account to retirement, in today's dollars, with zoom and life events
- Retirement income after tax, Social Security, and how long your savings last
- Balance history, CSV import and export, backups and update reminders

Your data stays on your computer. There's no account to create.
